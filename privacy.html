GROTH · PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated 25 October 2026.

Who uses GROTH

GROTH is a personal assistant used only by the person who built it. It has no other users and is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

Only with its owner's explicit permission, given on Google's consent screen, GROTH can access that owner's own Google account:

  • Google Calendar events, to read, add and change them;
  • Gmail messages, to read them and write drafts (sending always needs the owner's confirmation);
  • Google Docs and Sheets, to read and edit documents the owner asks about;
  • Google Drive, read-only, to find those files.

How the data is used and stored

Data is used only to carry out the owner's own requests. It is kept on the owner's own laptop and home server. To understand and answer a request, the relevant text is sent to Anthropic's Claude API, which processes it under Anthropic's commercial terms. It is not sold, shared with anyone else, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

GROTH's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, after which GROTH can no longer reach the account.
